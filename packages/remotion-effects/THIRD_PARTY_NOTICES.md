## Hyperframes shader transitions

The Clash cinematic transition effects `whip-pan`, `light-leak`, and
`flash-through-white` adapt transition concepts and shader logic from:

- Project: Hyperframes
- Source: https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/tree/main/packages/shader-transitions
- Copyright: Hyperframes contributors
- License: Apache License 2.0

The Clash versions were rewritten for WebGL2 / GLSL ES 3.0, integrated with
the Clash versioned effect schema, and expose different parameter and uniform
contracts. The original project license is available at:
https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/blob/main/LICENSE
